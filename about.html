This website is a working demonstration of a state-wide tourism portal for Karnataka. It covers all 31 districts with 524 destination records — 183 of them temples — 62 homestay listings, 31 guide profiles and 12 cultural experiences, and it implements the whole journey: search, filters, a map, a booking form, a simulated payment, a printable ticket and a booking history.

What is real and what is not

Destination records come from two places. 172 were written and checked by hand: coordinates geocoded against OpenStreetMap, descriptions compiled from public sources, fees and timings stated only where they could be confirmed. The other 352 were harvested from Wikidata and English Wikipedia — each one is a real place with its own Wikidata item, coordinates from that record, and a description taken from the article's lead paragraph. Harvested pages link back to their source article, and where the district was inferred from the coordinates rather than read from the administrative record, the page says so. Their entry fees and opening hours are marked "not verified" rather than guessed.

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons, with the author and licence stored per image. Entry fees and opening hours change often, so they appear as indicative notes with a reminder to confirm at the site. No fee or timing was invented to fill a field.

Homestays, guides and cultural experiences here are demonstration listings. They are labelled DEMO wherever they appear, no real property or person is represented, and nothing on this site is a real reservation. A real homestay directory needs the state tourism department's registered list, which is not published in a machine-readable form; those listings can be dropped in without code changes once they are available.

Booking

There is no account and no sign-in: browsing and booking are open to everyone. A booking needs only the name, email and phone you type on the form, and the record is kept in your own browser.

Mobile OTP sign-in was built and then removed at the owner's request. The code is still in the repository — assets/js/auth.js plus the otp-service/ worker and the Flask routes — so it can be switched back on later without rewriting it.

Payments

Every payment screen is simulated. No payment gateway is connected, nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, and the transaction IDs are generated in your browser. The screens say so, deliberately, on every page.

Where your bookings live

This is a static website: there is no server and no database. Bookings are saved in your own browser's local storage, which means they are visible only on this device and disappear if you clear site data.

Languages and themes

The interface is available in English, हिन्दी and ಕನ್ನಡ, and your choice is remembered. Six headline descriptions also have checked Hindi and Kannada translations; where a translation does not exist the page says so instead of showing English as if it were translated. Light and dark themes share one contrast-checked palette, and the choice is remembered too.

Technology

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step and no framework. Leaflet with OpenStreetMap for the map, qrcode-generator for the demo ticket QR, and localStorage for the booking flow. The data is generated from a single JSON file, so adding a place is a data change, not a code change.
